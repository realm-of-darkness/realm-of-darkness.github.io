The End

Freedom!

The key turns with a satisfying clunk. The gate swings open. You stagger out into the night, covered in dust, cobwebs and a light dusting of somebody else.

It is the 31st of October. The street is full of people dressed as ghosts, zombies and vampires. “Amazing costume!” someone shouts. “So realistic! How did you get the grave smell?” You win the costume contest. The prize is a pumpkin. You have never been happier.

You escaped the crypt. The skulls are mildly impressed.
