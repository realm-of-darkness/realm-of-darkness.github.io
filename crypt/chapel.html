Chapter V

The Bat Chapel

The ledge ends in a tiny chapel. The ceiling is alive: thousands of bats hang there like a velvet curtain, breathing softly. A single candle burns on the altar, the only light left in the whole crypt.

In the corner stands an ancient organ, its keys yellow as old teeth. Down by your feet, one dusty pedal looks as if nobody has touched it in centuries. A cool draught flows from a passage on the far side. A few bats flutter quietly in that direction.

  • Shout “Hello? Anyone?”
  • Play something on the organ
  • Blow out the candle and make a wish
  • Follow the bats, very quietly
  • Go back along the ledge
