The Museum of Petty Curses

Not every curse is dramatic. Most don't involve pharaohs or cursed rings. Most are small, petty and unbearably persistent. Here we collect, catalogue and display them, so that you may know your enemy.

Curse of the Day

The Collection

Please do not touch the exhibits. Several of them are already touching you.

Exhibit No. 1

The Short Charger Cable

Discovered: c. 2007 · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Always exactly 20 cm too short to reach the bed. Scientists have tried longer cables. The curse adapts.

Exhibit No. 2

The Wobbly Shopping Cart

Discovered: Medieval period · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Of all the carts in the row, you will choose the one with the haunted wheel. It wants to go left. It always wants to go left.

Exhibit No. 3

Eternal 1% Battery

Discovered: 2011 · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Your phone reaches 1% at the precise moment you need the map. It then lasts exactly long enough to show you how lost you are.

Exhibit No. 4

The Song Stuck in Your Head

Discovered: Prehistoric · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Not a good song. Never a good song. Usually a jingle for a furniture store that closed in 1998.

Exhibit No. 5

The Sock Abyss

Discovered: Since the invention of socks · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Two socks enter the washing machine. One sock leaves. Where the other goes, no living mortal knows.

Exhibit No. 6

The USB Stick, Upside Down

Discovered: 1996 · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Inserted wrong. Flipped. Still wrong. Flipped again. Correct. This exhibit has been rotated three times by the curator.

Exhibit No. 7

The Slowest Queue

Discovered: Ancient Egypt · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Whichever queue you join becomes the slowest. Switching queues transfers the curse with you, like a loyal, terrible dog.

Exhibit No. 8

The Elevator Goodbye

Discovered: 1890s · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

You say goodbye to a colleague. You both walk the same direction. You meet again at the lift. Nobody knows what to say. Forever.

Exhibit No. 9

The Phantom Vibration

Discovered: 2009 · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Your phone buzzed in your pocket. It did not. It never does. The phone is in the other room.

Exhibit No. 10

The Lego Underfoot

Discovered: 1958 · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Laid by unseen hands at the exact spot your bare foot will land at 3 a.m. Exhibit behind glass for visitor safety.

Exhibit No. 11

The Unopenable Packet

Discovered: Industrial Revolution · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

‘Tear here.’ You tear there. Nothing tears. You resort to teeth, scissors and, eventually, prayer.

Exhibit No. 12

The Reply-All

Discovered: 1971 · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

One person replies all. Twelve people reply all asking to be removed. The curse feeds on every message.

Exhibit No. 13

The Name You Forgot

Discovered: Since names · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

They remember yours. They use it warmly. It is far too late to ask theirs. You will pretend for the next eleven years.

Exhibit No. 14

The Hot Side of the Pillow

Discovered: Classical antiquity · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

You flip the pillow to the cool side. Within seconds it, too, becomes the hot side. There is no cool side. There never was.

Exhibit No. 15

The Bus That Leaves

Discovered: 1830s · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Departs the very moment you arrive at the stop, close enough to see the driver's face. The driver smiles. The driver knows.

Exhibit No. 16

The Tangled Earphones

Discovered: 1979 · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Placed neatly in a pocket. Removed as a single cursed knot of Gordian complexity. Wireless earphones replaced this with Curse No. 19.

Exhibit No. 17

The Restaurant Order Envy

Discovered: Roman era · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Your food arrives. It is fine. Then their food arrives, and you understand you have chosen wrong. You always will.

Exhibit No. 18

The Update Required

Discovered: 1995 · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Your device must update right now. It will take 47 minutes. It will change one icon, slightly, for the worse.

Exhibit No. 19

The Lost Single Earbud

Discovered: 2016 · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

One earbud, gone. Possibly in the sofa. Possibly in the Sock Abyss (see No. 5). Possibly ascended.

Exhibit No. 20

The Spoon in the Disposal

Discovered: 1940s · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Something is in the sink. It is always a spoon. It is always there. The spoon is the true owner of your kitchen.

Exhibit No. 21

The Cold Coffee

Discovered: 17th century · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Made with love. Set down for just a second. Discovered stone cold 40 minutes later. The second cup will suffer the same fate.

Exhibit No. 22

The Inconvenient Sneeze

Discovered: Since noses · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Arrives only during silence: at funerals, exams, job interviews and the quiet bit of a film.

Exhibit No. 23

The Password Requirements

Discovered: 2003 · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Must contain a capital, a number, a symbol, a rune and a drop of blood. Must not resemble any password you can remember.

Exhibit No. 24

The Stair That Isn't There

Discovered: Gothic period · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

In the dark, your foot finds a stair that does not exist. Your heart leaves your body briefly. It returns, but changed.

Exhibit No. 25

The Gift of Socks

Discovered: Victorian era · Severity ☠☠☠☠☠

Not technically a curse. Just a lifelong, gentle disappointment. Kept in the museum for its dark sentimental value.
